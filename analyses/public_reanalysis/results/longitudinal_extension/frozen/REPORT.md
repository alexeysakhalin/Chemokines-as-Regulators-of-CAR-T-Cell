# Longitudinal CXCR6 public-cohort extension

## Result

The patient was the independent unit, and each cohort was analyzed separately. GSE197268 showed a positive median change from infusion product to enriched day-7 CAR-T cells (+23.63 percentage points; 7/9 patients increased; exact sign-test P=0.1797; Holm P=0.5391). GSE235760 also showed a positive but smaller median change from manufactured varni-cel to the patient-specific expansion peak (+1.36 percentage points; 4/5 increased; P=0.3750; Holm P=0.7500). In contrast, the GSE162975 early comparison was close to zero and negative in median (−0.93 percentage points; 4/10 increased; P=0.7539; Holm P=0.7539). Thus, none of the three early tests had Holm-adjusted P<0.05, and the public data do not support a universal early CXCR6 increase.

Later comparisons were directionally lower but exploratory: the first GSE162975 T3/T4.5 stage versus T1 had a median change of −15.70 percentage points (6/7 decreased; P=0.125), the first T6-or-later stage versus T1 had a median change of −1.60 percentage points (3/5 decreased; P=1.000), and GSE273170 day 14 versus day 7 had a median change of −14.70 percentage points among the four pairs passing the 10-cell rule (3/4 decreased; P=0.625).

Within GSE197268, the product-stratified summaries were descriptive: all five axi-cel pairs increased (median +26.12 percentage points; P=0.0625), whereas two of four tisa-cel pairs increased (median −0.73 percentage points; P=1.000). These small strata do not support a time-by-product interaction test.

## Interpretation boundary

These observations support temporal and study-context heterogeneity of CXCR6 transcript detection. They do not establish trafficking, chemotaxis, retention, egress, a CXCL16 protein gradient, soluble-versus-membrane CXCL16 biology, efficacy, neurotoxicity, or causality. Product-stratified estimates are descriptive and do not constitute a time-by-product interaction test.

## Frozen-audit boundary

Exact patient rows are committed for GSE197268, GSE235760, GSE162975, and GSE273170. For GSE197268, an outcome-blind annotation screen identified nine of 21 paired patients with at least 25 eligible CAR-positive CD8 T cells at both stages; all 21 inclusion decisions are committed. The nine patient rows were reconstructed directly from their 18 checksum-pinned GEO 10x integer-count bundles joined to two checksum-pinned author cell-annotation files. CXCR6 positivity required a raw integer count greater than zero. Source files remain at their original repositories and are not redistributed here.
